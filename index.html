    Welcome aboard

    Sixty North helps you study or work in calm, steady blocks. Every full hour you focus moves you one stop further on a video trip across Canada. Go at your own pace. Every minute counts.

    Why is it called Sixty North?

    For two reasons. One focus session is 60 minutes. And the 60th parallel is the line on the map where British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan and Manitoba end and the territories begin. You are heading north, one hour at a time.

    The rhythm: 60 / 10 / 40

    Focus for 60 minutes, rest for 10. After four focus sessions, take a long 40-minute break. You can change all of these in Settings.

    How to do one round

    1. Add a task. On Home, press Add task and write what you will work on. Tap a task to make it your current one.
    2. Press START. Focus on that one task for 60 minutes. Put your phone face down if you can.
    3. Hear the anthem, take a break. When time is up, O Canada plays. Stand up, stretch, drink some water.
    4. Repeat. After four focus sessions you get a long 40-minute break. You earned it.

    Unlocking the trip

    Your focus minutes add up across days. Every time they reach another full hour, a new stop unlocks: hour 1 is Banff, hour 2 is Vancouver, and so on, all the way to Nova Scotia at hour 29. At hour 30 you unlock the outro and Chris Hadfield's full talk on how you can achieve your goals. The intro and "About Canada" are free, so you can watch them right away.

    If you stop a focus session early, the minutes you did still count, as long as you focused for at least one minute. Unlocked stops stay unlocked. Videos play in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).

    Changing the timer

    • Open Settings (top right) to change focus and break lengths, pick a preset, turn on auto-start, set a daily goal, and change the anthem's volume.
    • Tap the big clock to type a new time for this session only.
    • Use −5 and +5 to shorten or stretch the current session.
    • ↺ restarts the session. ⏭ finishes it now and moves on.

    Exploring the map

    Tap a province or territory to read its story, see its flag and quick facts, and open photos. In the photo viewer you can zoom with the + and − buttons, the mouse wheel, a pinch on your phone, or a double tap. Drag to look around. Want a challenge? Press Play: find the province.

    Your study report

    The Report tab shows how much you focused today, this week and over time, which tasks got your time, and every session you finished. You can download your history or save a backup file and restore it on another computer.

    Keeping the timer on time

    The timer follows the real clock, not a counter, so it stays accurate when you switch tabs or minimize the window. A few tips:

    • Keep this tab open. Minimizing is fine; closing the tab is not.
    • Press START once before you leave the tab so your browser allows the anthem to play.
    • Turn on notifications in Settings for a pop-up when time is up.
    • If your computer goes to sleep, the session ends when you come back and the timer waits for you instead of running on.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    • Space start or pause
    • Esc close a window
    • Photo viewer: ← → change photo, + − zoom, 0 fit to screen

    Your data

    Everything you do here is saved in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data will erase it, so save a backup from the Report tab now and then.

    For the hard days

    Some days one focus session is a big win. That is okay. Resting is part of the plan, not a break from it. Come back tomorrow, press START, and keep paddling. Canada is a big country, and nobody crosses it in one day.
